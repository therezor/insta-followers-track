Working

See who unfollowed you

Press Scan now. Follower Tracker reads your own follower and following lists from the Instagram tab you are already logged into, and keeps them on this computer.

  1. Make sure you are logged in at instagram.com.
  2. Press Scan now and leave the tab open.
  3. Scan again in a few days — the change lists fill in from the second scan onwards.

No account, no password, no follower limit. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Everything is computed and stored in this browser, on this computer. Follower Tracker sends nothing to any server except instagram.com itself, and it has no analytics of any kind.
